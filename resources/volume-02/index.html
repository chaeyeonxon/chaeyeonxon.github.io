Inference, Selection, and Simulation

This book connects population arguments to sampling evidence. It covers conditioning, randomized contrasts, partial identification, competing rates, extremum estimators, simulation error, generated objects, spectral uncertainty, dependent-data covariance, bootstrap frontiers, and out-of-time validation.
